AlphaSync Help

Get started with AlphaSync

AlphaSync copies files between your Mac and an AlphaSmart Neo. An applet is a program on your Neo, such as AlphaWord.

Connect and find your files

  1. Put AlphaSync in your Mac’s Applications folder and open it.
  2. Connect your Neo with a USB cable. If macOS asks whether to allow the accessory, review and allow your Neo.
  3. Choose All Files beneath your Neo in the sidebar, or choose an applet to see its files.
  4. Double-click a file to read it, or choose File → Save to Mac… to save a copy.

AlphaSync handles the connection for file transfers. You do not need to send your writing as keyboard input.

To choose which types of files appear, use AlphaSync → Settings… → Show. Changing this filter does not remove anything from your Neo.

Make a first backup

Click Backup. It saves the included files from every connected Neo. Check the result, then choose Open Backup Folder to find the saved files. Make an independent backup before replacing or clearing important writing.

Use more than one Neo

Each connected Neo has its own sidebar section. Choose the intended Neo and applet before copying or changing files. A warning about another Neo does not hide the files of one that is working.

Check your Mac version

AlphaSync 2.0.4 requires macOS 13 or later. Its Universal edition supports Intel and Apple Silicon; the Apple Silicon edition supports M-series Macs. Install one edition. For macOS 11 or 12, use the legacy 1.5.1 release (online).
