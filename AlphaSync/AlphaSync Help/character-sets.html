Text formats and character sets

Files saved to your Mac use UTF-8 without a byte-order mark and ordinary LF line endings. Imports accept UTF-8, UTF-16 with a byte-order mark in either byte order, and MacASCII (Mac Roman).

The Neo stores a smaller character set than Unicode. AlphaSync converts supported characters back into the Neo’s format. Pound signs, supported accents, and curly quotes can be preserved. Unsupported characters, including many emoji and mathematical symbols, stop the import with an explanation.

No silent replacement is made. Edit the source text to remove or replace the named character, then import again. Windows, Mac, and Unix paragraph endings are converted consistently.

The Neo’s size limit applies to converted device bytes. A UTF-8 file’s size on your Mac can differ from its size on the Neo. Shorten or split oversized text.
