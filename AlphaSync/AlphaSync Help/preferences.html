AlphaSync Help

Settings

Choose AlphaSync → Settings….

Files shown and backed up

Choose AlphaWord files, AlphaWord and dictionary files, or all applet files. The same filter controls the main list and backups.

Backups

Choose where backups and recovery copies are saved. Backup logs are off unless you enable Save a backup log. Exported text always uses UTF-8.

Recovery copies

Save recovery copies by default is initially on. A Replace or Clear warning can override that choice for its own operation. Keep copies for 7, 30, or 90 days, or until you remove them. The default is 30 days.

Open Recovery Copies opens the folder inside your backup destination. It is created when you first save a recovery copy. Restore text by importing its saved .txt file onto the matching Neo file. Keep the raw .neo files for recovery or diagnosis.

Only recognised, complete expired copies move to Trash, when a new copy is made. Incomplete and unrelated folders are left alone.

Automatic opening

Enable Open AlphaSync when a Neo is connected. The separate status explains whether Login Items approval is required. Use Open Login Items Settings to review the system’s setting.
