AlphaSync Help

This development version requires macOS 13 or later.

AlphaSync copies text files between your Mac and an AlphaSmart Neo. You can also read files in a separate window and back up your Neo’s files.

Getting Started

  1. Open AlphaSync and connect your Neo with a USB cable.
  2. Double-click a file to read it. You can scroll and copy text in its read-only window.
  3. Drag files to a Finder folder to save them on your Mac, or click Backup to back up your files.

Back up your files before replacing or clearing them on the Neo.
