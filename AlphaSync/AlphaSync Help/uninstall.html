AlphaSync Help

Remove AlphaSync from your Mac

  1. Open AlphaSync → Settings… and turn off Open AlphaSync when a Neo is connected.
  2. Wait for active file work to finish, then choose AlphaSync → Quit AlphaSync.
  3. In Finder, move AlphaSync from Applications to Trash.

Your copied text, backups, and recovery copies stay in their chosen folders. Removing the app does not clear the writing on your Neo. Check saved files before removing any copies you no longer need.

The bundled helper belongs inside the app; you do not need to remove it separately. If you reinstall AlphaSync, review Settings before making changes to files.
