Uninstalling AlphaSync

  1. Open Settings and turn off automatic opening.
  2. Quit AlphaSync. Active device work finishes before the app closes.
  3. Move AlphaSync from Applications to Trash.

Your saved text, backups, and recovery copies remain in their chosen folders. Remove them separately only after checking that you no longer need them. Keep any backups you want to preserve.
