AlphaSync Help

Restore text from a recovery copy

Recovery copies keep the original writing before you replace or clear a file. They are separate from a full backup and are on by default.

Find a saved original

  1. Choose AlphaSync → Settings….
  2. Under Recovery copies, choose Open Recovery Copies.
  3. Open the dated folder for the change you want to recover. Read the saved text to check that it is the original you need.

The folder is inside your chosen backup destination. It is created when you first save a recovery copy, so the button may report that no folder exists before then.

Put the text back on your Neo

  1. Connect your Neo and select the correct file in the correct applet.
  2. Choose File → Import Text Files to Neo… and choose the saved .txt file.
  3. Check the file named in the Replace warning. Keep a recovery copy of its current contents if you need them.
  4. Choose Replace and wait for writing and verification to finish.

Check the destination: restoring text replaces the current contents of the selected Neo file. AlphaSync does not provide a one-click restore or Edit → Undo for device changes.

Keep the accompanying .neo files. They preserve the original Neo data for recovery or diagnosis, but they are not plain-text imports. Other applet .xml copies cannot be imported back in this version.

Choose how long to keep copies

In Settings, Keep copies offers 7, 30, or 90 days, or Until I remove them. The default is 30 days. Complete, recognised expired copies move to Trash when a new recovery copy is made. Incomplete copies and unrelated folders are left alone.

Keep an independent backup of important writing, especially before removing saved originals or emptying Trash.
