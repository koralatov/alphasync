# AlphaSync 2.0.2 – archived releases

Both 2.0.2 releases are superseded by **[AlphaSync 2.0.3](2.0.3.md)** for macOS 13 and later. Use its release page for current downloads, installation, everyday use, and technical details.

The original [build 151 notes](verification/2.0.2-151/notes.md) and [build 152 notes](verification/2.0.2-152/notes.md), asset hashes, and verification records remain archived. The `v2.0.2` and `v2.0.2-build.152` source tags are preserved. Their release listings are retired after 2.0.3 is verified and public.

For macOS 11 and 12, use **[AlphaSync 1.5.1](https://github.com/koralatov/alphasync/releases/tag/v1.5.1)**. Its source, legacy branch, and original downloads remain unchanged.
